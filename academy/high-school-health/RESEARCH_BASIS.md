## SHAPE America 2024 National Physical Education Standards

The current National PE Standards were released in March 2024.

The four current PE standards are represented in:
`courses/physical-education/standards-2024.json`

Source:
https://www.shapeamerica.org/standards/pe/new-pe-standards.aspx

High-school indicators emphasize lifetime activities, recreational games, outdoor pursuits, dance, cardiovascular/strength/flexibility training, fitness planning, RPE, heart-rate concepts, rest, safe participation, teamwork, leadership, and meaningful post-high-school movement choices.

National Physical Education Standards are used under license from SHAPE America.

## SHAPE America 2024 National Health Education Standards

The current National Health Education Standards were released in March 2024.

The eight standards emphasize:
- functional health information;
- influences;
- reliable resources;
- communication;
- decision making;
- goal setting;
- health practices;
- advocacy.

Source:
https://www.shapeamerica.org/standards/health/new-he-standards.aspx

National Health Education Standards are used under license from SHAPE America.

## CDC physical activity guidance

CDC currently summarizes the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans as recommending 60 minutes or more of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity daily for youth ages 6–17, including vigorous, muscle-strengthening, and bone-strengthening activity on at least 3 days per week.

Source:
https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-education/guidelines/index.html
