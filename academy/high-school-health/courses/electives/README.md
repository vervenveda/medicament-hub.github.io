# Khaemenes High Electives

This is a federated catalog.

High School may list and route students to specialist repositories without copying their course files or claiming Mentor ownership.

Current categories include visual arts/design, music/recording arts, languages/linguistics, atmospheric/Earth sciences, law/legal studies, health/medical studies, business/finance, environmental service learning, archive/research, professional tools, civic learning, and information/media literacy.

`federation-map.json` is the machine-readable source for the landing page inventory.

Credit is determined by documented course scope and applicable local policy, not by directory listing alone.
