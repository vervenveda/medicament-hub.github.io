# Khaemenes High — Health & Wellness

Repair #8 establishes four skills-based Health Education courses:

- Grade 9 — Personal Health Foundations
- Grade 10 — Relationships, Safety & Decision-Making
- Grade 11 — Community Health, Media & Systems
- Grade 12 — Lifelong Health & Independent Living

Each course is 18 weeks / 90 lesson blocks.

The courses use the eight 2024 SHAPE America National Health Education Standards as a skills framework.

National Health Education Standards are used under license from SHAPE America.

Source:
https://www.shapeamerica.org/standards/health/new-he-standards.aspx

## Privacy

Students are not required to disclose private diagnoses, trauma, sexuality, family medical history, substance use, or other sensitive personal health details. Use fictional, public, or self-selected non-sensitive scenarios.

## Medical boundary

This is health education, not medical care. Courses do not diagnose, prescribe, or replace qualified professional care.

## Credit

Jurisdiction-specific review is required before formal transcript or graduation-credit claims.
