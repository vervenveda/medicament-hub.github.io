Khaemenes High · Grade 12

Lifelong Health & Independent Living 12

Skills-based health education aligned to the 2024 SHAPE America National Health Education Standards. This course teaches health literacy and decision skills; it does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace qualified care.

Privacy rule: assignments use fictional, public, or self-selected non-sensitive scenarios. Students should not be required to disclose diagnoses, trauma, sexuality, family medical history, substance use, or other private health information.
