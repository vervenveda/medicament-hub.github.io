# Khaemenes High School Physical Education

## Canonical ownership

Repair #8 migrates the existing High School PE assets from:

`vervenveda/medicament-hub.github.io/academy/`

to:

`vervenveda/Khaemenes_High.github.io/courses/physical-education/`

The Medicament files are historical/source material. Khaemenes High becomes the academic owner of the High School PE curriculum.

## Preserved assets

The migration workflow copies, byte-for-byte:

- the 42-week High School PE portal;
- the PE Teacher Guide;
- Grade 9 study guide + quizzes;
- Grade 10 study guide + quizzes;
- Grade 11 study guide + quizzes;
- Grade 12 study guide + quizzes.

The PLERA PE Mini Textbook Reader supplied separately is included directly at:

`courses/physical-education/reader/index.html`

## Modernization strategy

The existing PE portal uses the older five-standard SHAPE model. Repair #8 deliberately does **not** edit those source files during migration.

Instead, `standards-2024.json` provides a clean modern crosswalk to the 2024 four-standard National Physical Education Standards.

This prevents a standards update from accidentally breaking a working curriculum portal.

## National reference points

SHAPE America:
https://www.shapeamerica.org/standards/pe/new-pe-standards.aspx

CDC youth physical activity guidelines:
https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-education/guidelines/index.html

National Physical Education Standards are used under license from SHAPE America.

## Credit note

State and local PE/Health credit requirements differ. The course structure supports transcript evidence, but formal credit/graduation reporting requires local review.
