How to use this tool wisely:
  • Choose a “pattern” you can keep for months, not days.
  • Use structure (plate method + routines) before restriction.
  • Quality matters: whole foods & fiber usually outperform ultra-processed options.
  • For IBS or major symptoms: elimination diets are best done with a trained clinician/dietitian.
